<Note>
Paid apps open in stages after launch. Until they are open, versions with a price can be created but paid apps are not shown in the app store.
</Note>

## Requirements

- A **public** app. Paid apps are sold through the app store listing.
- **Identity verification (KYC)**: your seller account must be approved before a store can buy your app. It is the same seller account you use to sell themes. Without approval, the purchase is refused.

## Pricing

A paid app has a **one-time** price per store. Set both currencies in the version:

```json
{ "pricing": { "model": "one_time", "priceTry": 499, "priceUsd": 15 } }
```

| | Limit |
| --- | --- |
| `priceTry` | Up to 100,000 TRY |
| `priceUsd` | Up to 5,000 USD |

The price is part of the version, so a price change goes through review like any other change. Subscriptions are not available yet.

## Licenses

- A store buys the app once; the license belongs to the **store**. Payment is handled by CrafterPayments.
- The store needs an active license to install a paid app. Without one, the install stops with `402 app_license_required`.
- Uninstalling does **not** refund. The license stays with the store, so it can install the app again without paying.
- A refund or a lost chargeback revokes the license and disables the installation: the app's tokens stop working.

## Revenue share

Crafter keeps **10%** of each sale, calculated on the amount excluding tax. The rest is credited to your seller balance and paid out like theme sales. You see app sales in **Developer > Earnings**.

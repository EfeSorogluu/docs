CrafterPayments is a fully integrated payment system built for game servers. Without the need to set up a company, it lets you accept Visa, Mastercard and Troy cards from all over the world instantly, protected by 3D Secure.

Go to [Dashboard \> Settings \> Payment \> Crafter Payments](https://app.crafter.net.tr/en/dashboard/settings/payment/crafter-payments) to enable CrafterPayments, submit your KYC details and manage your earnings.

## Key Benefits

Unlike traditional external POS providers, CrafterPayments does not require days of paperwork or a technical integration (API keys, webhooks and so on).

- **No company required:** No tax registration or accounting overhead. Individuals can be approved within minutes (a parent or guardian IBAN is supported for users under 18).
- **Card acceptance worldwide:** Accept payments from players in Turkey and abroad.
- **Zero code, ready instantly:** No API keys or secret tokens to manage. It is enabled on your store with a single click.
- **Fast settlement:** Withdraw your earnings with a T\+7 day settlement period.

---

## Application and KYC

To accept payments through CrafterPayments and transfer your earnings to your bank account, you need to submit your identity and bank details (KYC).

### 1. Bank and Identity (KYC) Details

Click **"Define KYC and Bank Details"** in the dashboard and fill in the following:

- **Full Name / Company Title:** Your name as it appears on your ID.
- **T.C. Identity Number:** Your 11-digit Turkish identity number.
- **IBAN:** The bank account, starting with TR, that payouts will be sent to.

_Once approved, these details are locked for security. The IBAN holder must match the identity number and name or company title._

## Balance and Withdrawals

The CrafterPayments dashboard tracks your finances with 4 main metrics:

- **Withdrawable Balance:** The net amount that has completed the T\+7 settlement period and can be withdrawn to your bank account (minimum withdrawal 50 ₺).
- **Pending Settlement:** Revenue collected from your customers that has not yet completed the T\+7 day holding period.
- **Total Service Revenue:** All-time net revenue earned through Crafter POS.
- **Total Withdrawn:** The total amount successfully transferred to your bank account so far.

<Note>
  **Pass the commission on to the customer:** You can choose to pass the standard 5.99% transaction fee on to your customer with one click. When enabled, the fee is added to the payment as a transaction fee and you receive 100% of the sale amount.
</Note>

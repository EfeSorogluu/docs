## Setup & Configuration

<Steps>
  <Step title="Navigate to Turnstile Menu">
    Log in to your [Cloudflare Dashboard](https://dash.cloudflare.com/) and select **Turnstile** from the sidebar menu.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create a New Widget">
    Click **Add Widget** and fill in the required fields:

    - **Widget Name:** Enter `Crafter CMS Widget`.
    - **Domains (Hostnames):** Use the `Add a custom hostname` option to add `crafter.net.tr` first, followed by your own domain (e.g., `yourdomain.com` and `www.yourdomain.com`).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Copy API Keys">
    Click **Create** to save. Copy the generated **Site Key** and **Secret Key**.

    <Frame caption="Cloudflare Turnstile Widget API Keys Screen">
      ![Cloudflare Turnstile Site Key and Secret Key](/images/turnstile-keys-overview.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure in Crafter CMS">
    1. Go to [Dashboard \> Settings \> Security](https://app.crafter.net.tr/en/dashboard/settings/security).
    2. Toggle on the **Enable Cloudflare Turnstile** option.
    3. Paste your **Site Key** and **Secret Key** into the respective fields and save changes.

    <Frame caption="Crafter CMS Security and Turnstile Settings">
      ![Crafter CMS Turnstile Configuration Screen](/images/crafter-turnstile-settings.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Widget Not Showing">
    - Verify that you have entered the correct **Site Key** in the Crafter Control Panel.
    - Check that your domain name is added to the allowed hostnames list in Cloudflare Dashboard.
    - Clear your browser cache and refresh the page.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Validation Errors">
    - Ensure the **Secret Key** is correctly entered without missing characters.
    - Verify that your server can establish outbound connections to Cloudflare APIs.
    - Inspect the logs in your Crafter Dashboard for detailed error payloads.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Domain Not Working">
    - Ensure all domains and subdomains (`www` and apex domains) are explicitly listed in the widget's hostname settings.
    - Allow a few minutes for new hostnames to propagate across Cloudflare's network.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

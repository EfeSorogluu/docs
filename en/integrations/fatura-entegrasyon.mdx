Crafter CMS supports automated e-invoice and digital tax compliance integrations for all digital gaming store purchases and balance top-ups.

---

## Supported Invoicing Gateways

- **BirFatura**

---

## Configuration Workflow

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Invoice Settings">
    Navigate to [Dashboard \> Settings \> Invoice](https://app.crafter.net.tr/tr/dashboard/settings/invoice).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Provide Company Details">
    Fill in your registered legal entity title, tax office, tax registration ID, and legal address.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect API">
    Insert your integrator API token and toggle on **Automatic Invoice Generation**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

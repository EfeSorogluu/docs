On Crafter, a plan is bought per **account (workspace)**, not per store. An account holds your stores, your team and your plan together.

## Account (workspace)

- Each user can have one free account. A new account always starts on the **Free** plan.
- You can open a second account with a paid plan; it stays pending until the first payment, and no stores or members can be added in the meantime.
- The number of stores in an account is limited by your plan's store allowance.
- You can move a store you own to another of your accounts if you are an owner or admin in both. After the move, the new account's plan and commission apply to **later** transactions.

## Team

Add existing Crafter accounts to your account as team members by email. The number of members (owner included) cannot exceed your plan's staff allowance.

| Role | What they can do |
| --- | --- |
| Owner | Everything: plan, billing, team and all stores. |
| Admin | Manages the team and stores. Can be limited to some stores. |
| Member | Manages only the stores given to them, according to their role in each store. |

Only owners and admins see the last 30 days of revenue on the overview.

## Plans

There are three plans: **Free**, **Pro** and **Business**. They differ in features such as the number of stores, team members, domains, branding, themes, community modules, game integrations, analytics, email, AI translation, webhooks, API keys, headless payment limits and the CrafterPayments [commission](/en/crafterpayments/fees).

Current plan contents and prices: [pricing page](https://crafter.net.tr/en/pricing).

<Note>
When you try to use a feature that is not in your plan, the dashboard shows which plan you need.
</Note>

### Extra stores

On the Business plan you can buy **extra stores** on top of the plan's store allowance. An increase takes effect right away (you pay the difference); a decrease takes effect at the end of the period.

## Billing

- Plans are paid monthly or yearly; yearly billing is discounted.
- The price is shown and charged in a currency based on the country of the card you pay with (for example EUR for the EU/EEA, GBP for the United Kingdom, TRY for Türkiye, USD for other countries). The subscription currency cannot be changed later.
- If a card renewal fails, it is retried; your plan stays active until the end of the period.

## Changing or canceling your plan

- **Upgrades** take effect right away; you pay the price difference for the rest of the period.
- **Downgrades** take effect at the end of the period. If your usage (number of stores or team members) is over the new plan's limit, you are asked to reduce it first.
- If you **cancel**, your plan stays active until the end of the period, then the account returns to Free.
- An account that is not renewed at the end of the period runs with Free features. If the account has more stores than Free allows, the extra stores fall outside the plan limit; the dashboard shows which stores are affected.

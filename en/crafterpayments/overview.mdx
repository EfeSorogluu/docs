---
title: "What is CrafterPayments?"
description: "The merchant of record model: Crafter Technologies LLC makes the sale, and you receive your earnings every week."
---

CrafterPayments is Crafter's own payment service. In the global model, the **merchant of record (MoR) for sales in your store is Crafter Technologies LLC**. This means:

- Crafter Technologies LLC collects the payment from the buyer as the seller.
- Tax on the sale (VAT / sales tax) is calculated on the payment page from the buyer's billing country and collected by Crafter as the seller.
- The buyer receives a receipt by email.
- Refund requests and chargebacks are handled through Crafter; you follow the process in the dashboard and provide proof of delivery when needed.
- Your earnings (the sale amount minus commission and processing fee) build up in your balance and are [sent to your bank account automatically every week](/en/crafterpayments/payouts).

<Note>
You do not need a company. You can apply as an individual or as a company. Application steps: [CrafterPayments application](/en/crafterpayments/onboarding).
</Note>

## What you are responsible for

- That the items you sell are legal and follow the game's rules (for example the Minecraft Usage Guidelines for Minecraft servers).
- Delivering the item to the buyer. Crafter's game integrations deliver automatically; failed deliveries show up in the dashboard.
- Buyer support for your store (questions about your items).

## How it works

1. The buyer builds a cart in your store (or on your own site with the [headless payments API](/en/developers/payments/overview)).
2. Payment is taken on CrafterPayments' secure payment page. The buyer sees and pays in their own currency. See [Multiple currencies](/en/crafterpayments/multi-currency).
3. When the payment is confirmed, the order is created and the item is delivered.
4. Your earnings are added to your balance in the store currency; amounts past the holding period go into the weekly payout.

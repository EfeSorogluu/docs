With CrafterPayments, two items are deducted from each sale; the rest is added to your balance.

| Item | How much |
| --- | --- |
| **Commission** | A percentage that depends on your plan. Current rates are on the [pricing page](https://crafter.net.tr/en/pricing). |
| **Processing fee** | A percentage per sale plus a fixed amount that depends on the currency. The current value is shown on the [pricing page](https://crafter.net.tr/en/pricing) and in the dashboard. |

<Note>
Rates come from the platform settings and can change over time. This page does not list fixed numbers; the value on the pricing page and in your dashboard always applies.
</Note>

## How the commission is set

- The commission is read from the plan of the account (workspace) the store belongs to.
- The rate is written to the transaction at payment time. When you change your plan or move the store to another account, the new rate applies to **later** sales; transactions already started keep the old rate.
- If a custom rate is set for your account, it is used instead of the plan rate.

## Processing fee

- Covers the cost of the payment infrastructure. It has a percentage part and a fixed part; the fixed part depends on the payment currency.
- The processing fee is not returned when a sale is refunded.
- The minimum for each card payment is the equivalent of 1 USD (1 unit in USD, EUR and GBP). Orders whose total is 0 with a coupon take no payment, so there is no processing fee.

---
title: "Multiple currencies"
description: "Store currency and customer currencies: buyers see and pay in their own currency, and the rate is locked before payment."
---

CrafterPayments has two currency concepts:

| | Store currency | Customer currency |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Used for | Your prices, balance, payouts and reports | The amount the buyer sees and pays |
| Options | USD, EUR, GBP or TRY | One of the supported currencies |
| Who chooses | You, when you set up the store | The system (the buyer can change it), within your setting |

## Store currency

You enter product prices in the store currency; accounting is kept in this currency. Once your store has sales or a balance, the currency can no longer be changed. If you need to change it, contact support.

## Customer currencies

Buyers see prices in their own currency and pay in that currency. You manage this under **Settings → Payments → Customer currencies** in the dashboard:

- **Automatic** (default for new stores): all supported currencies can be used.
- **Selected:** only the currencies you choose (plus the store currency) are shown.
- **Off:** buyers see only the store currency.

You can add an **exchange-rate markup** of 0 to 10 percent on top of the rate.

Supported customer currencies: USD, EUR, GBP, TRY, AUD, BRL, CAD, CHF, CNY, CZK, DKK, HKD, IDR, ILS, JPY, KRW, MXN, MYR, NOK, NZD, PHP, PLN, RON, SEK, SGD, THB, ZAR.

### How the currency is chosen

In order: the buyer's explicit choice (currency picker), IP country, browser language. If the country's currency is not on the list, the buyer sees the store currency.

### Locked rate

When the buyer moves to payment, the amount is converted at the current rate and **locked for 30 minutes**. If the buyer pays within that time, they pay exactly the amount they saw. If the time runs out or the cart changes, a new quote is made at the current rate.

Rates are updated daily from official sources. If no rate is available, only the store currency is used.

## What goes into your balance

Whatever currency the buyer pays in, the sale is recorded at the price in the store currency and your earnings are added to your balance in the store currency.

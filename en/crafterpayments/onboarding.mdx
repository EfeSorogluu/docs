---
title: "CrafterPayments application"
description: "Identity verification, seller details, bank account (Wise) and the US tax form; automatic approval and review."
---

To sell with CrafterPayments and receive your earnings, complete your application in the CrafterPayments section of **Settings → Payments** in the dashboard. The application has four steps; once they are done, it is usually approved automatically.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Identity verification">
    Verify your ID document and your face by following the on-screen steps. On a computer, you can continue on your phone by scanning a QR code. Details read from your ID, such as your name and date of birth, carry over to the next steps and cannot be edited.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Seller details">
    Fill in what your ID does not provide (for example your address or company details). Only missing fields are asked.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Bank account">
    Add the bank account your earnings will be sent to. Weekly payouts are sent to your bank account through **Wise**; the account details asked depend on your country and currency. Some details are filled in from the previous steps.
  </Step>
  <Step title="US tax form">
    Because the seller is a US company (Crafter Technologies LLC), you need to sign a US tax form. The dashboard picks the right form from your answers:

    - **W-9:** if you are a US person,
    - **W-8BEN:** if you are an individual outside the US,
    - **W-8BEN-E:** if you are a company outside the US.

    You fill in the form in the dashboard and sign it electronically. W-8 forms expire; you get an email reminder before that and renew the form in the dashboard.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Approval

When the four steps are done, the application is approved automatically. In these cases it goes to our team for review and you get the result by email:

- your identity verification needs a manual review,
- the sanctions (OFAC) screening finds a possible match, or your region is under sanctions,
- your store is suspended (the application is approved automatically once the suspension is lifted).

## After approval

For payouts to be sent, these must stay up to date; if something is missing, that week's payout is held and you get an email telling you what to do:

- accepting the current version of the CrafterPayments agreement,
- completed identity verification,
- a valid payout account (bank account),
- a valid tax form (W-9 / W-8),
- for high payouts, a document showing that the payout account is yours.

If you applied as a company, when a weekly payout is prepared you may be asked to upload your invoice for the amount as a PDF in the dashboard; the payout is sent once the invoice is uploaded.

See [Weekly payouts](/en/crafterpayments/payouts).

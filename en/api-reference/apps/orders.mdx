---
title: "Orders"
description: "List orders and retrieve one order, with payment and delivery status."
api: "GET https://api.crafterpayments.com/v1/orders"
playground: "none"
---

Scope: `read_orders`. Customer `name` and `email` are filled only when the token also has `read_customers`; otherwise they are `null`.

`GET /v1/orders` lists live orders, newest first. `livemode=false` lists test-mode orders instead; live and test orders are never mixed. `GET /v1/orders/{id}` accepts the order `id` or its `number` (the `data.orderId` of order webhooks) and returns orders of both modes, or `404 order_not_found`.

<ParamField query="limit" type="integer">1 to 100, default 10</ParamField>
<ParamField query="starting_after" type="string">Order id of the previous page's last item</ParamField>
<ParamField query="livemode" type="boolean">`false` for test-mode orders. Default `true`.</ParamField>
<ParamField query="status" type="string">`pending`, `paid`, `failed`, `canceled`, `refunded`</ParamField>
<ParamField query="customer" type="string">Only orders of this customer id</ParamField>

## The order object

<ResponseField name="id" type="string">Order (payment) id</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="object" type="string">Always `order`</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="livemode" type="boolean">`false` for a test-mode order</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="number" type="string">Order number shown in the panel; same as `data.orderId` in webhooks</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="status" type="string">`pending`, `paid`, `failed`, `canceled`, `refunded`</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="type" type="string">`cart` (products) or `balance` (store balance top-up)</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="amount" type="number">Amount charged, in `currency`</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="currency" type="string">ISO 4217</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="lineItems" type="object[]">`{ productId, name, quantity, unitAmount }`</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="customer" type="object">`{ id, name, email }`. `name` and `email` need `read_customers`. No phone number or address.</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="delivery" type="object">`{ status, deliveredAt, simulated }`. `status`: `pending`, `delivered`, `needs_review`, `failed`, `refunded`, or `null` when the order has no tracked delivery. `simulated` is `true` for test orders (nothing was delivered in game).</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="checkoutSessionId" type="string | null">When the order came from a checkout session</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="createdAt" type="string | null">ISO 8601</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="updatedAt" type="string | null">ISO 8601</ResponseField>

---
title: "Retrieve a session"
description: "Returns every field of a checkout session. Use it as the source of truth before completing an order."
api: "GET https://api.crafterpayments.com/v1/checkout/sessions/{id}"
playground: "none"
---

Requires a secret key. A key only sees sessions of its own store and its own mode; others return `404 checkout_session_not_found`.

An open session past its expiry is returned as `expired`, even if the background job has not closed it yet.

<ParamField path="id" type="string" required>
  Session id, for example `cs_live_…`.
</ParamField>

<ParamField header="Authorization" type="string" required>
  `Bearer cp_sk_…`
</ParamField>

## The session object

<ResponseField name="id" type="string">`cs_test_…` or `cs_live_…`</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="object" type="string">Always `checkout.session`</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="livemode" type="boolean">`true` in live mode</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="status" type="string">`open`, `processing`, `complete`, `expired`, `canceled`</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="paymentStatus" type="string">`unpaid`, `paid`, `no_payment_required`, `refunded`, `partially_refunded`, `disputed`</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="uiMode" type="string">`hosted` or `embedded`</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="url" type="string">Payment page: `https://checkout.crafterpayments.com/c/{id}`</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="clientSecret" type="string">For the `/public` read with the publishable key and for embedded mode. Do not log it.</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="expiresAt" type="string">ISO 8601</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="createdAt" type="string">ISO 8601</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="storeCurrency" type="string">The store currency, for example `USD`</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="amountSubtotal" type="number">Sum of the lines at catalog price in `storeCurrency` (before coupon and tax)</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="amountTotal" type="number | null">Final amount charged (tax included, after coupon). `null` when the session is created; filled after payment starts.</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="currency" type="string | null">Charge currency (the buyer's currency). `null` until payment starts.</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="lineItems" type="object[]">`{ productId, name, quantity, unitAmount, currency }`. `unitAmount` is the catalog unit price after product discount.</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="customer" type="object | null">`{ email, externalId, player }`</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="clientReferenceId" type="string | null" />
<ResponseField name="metadata" type="object">The metadata you sent; `{}` if none</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="orderId" type="string | null">The order number in the store once complete</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="paymentId" type="string | null">The payment record once complete (`null` for an order made free by a coupon)</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="completedAt" type="string | null">ISO 8601</ResponseField>

<RequestExample>

```bash curl
curl https://api.crafterpayments.com/v1/checkout/sessions/cs_live_8fK2... \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $CRAFTER_SECRET_KEY" \
  -H "Crafter-Version: 2026-12"
```

</RequestExample>
